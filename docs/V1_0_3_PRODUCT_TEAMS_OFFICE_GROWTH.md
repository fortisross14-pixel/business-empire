# Business Empire v1.0.3 — Product Teams & Office Growth

## Product project classes

### A
- 1 Product Designer
- ~35 base development days
- 13 design-priority points
- design quality capped around the 1–2★ range
- available to the startup organization

### AA
- 1 Product Lead + 1 Product Designer
- ~80 base development days
- 18 design-priority points
- capable of strong 3–4★ design
- requires an 8-seat Normal Office or larger
- team effectiveness: Lead 60%, Designer 40%

### AAA
- 1 Product Lead + 3 Product Designers
- ~150 base development days
- 23 design-priority points
- potential 5★ / market-leading design
- requires a 16-seat Large Office or larger
- team effectiveness: Lead 45%, three Designers collectively 55%

Project scale creates capacity, not guaranteed success. A poor Lead or weak team can waste the time and organizational cost of a AAA program.

## Product people

Product career path:

Product Designer → Senior Product Designer → Product Lead → VP Product

People assigned to a designing SKU are unavailable for another product until that design finishes. The Lead's quality therefore becomes a meaningful portfolio allocation decision.

## Office progression

- Small Suburban Office: 4 seats
- Normal Office: 8 seats
- Large Office: 16 seats
- Massive Corporate HQ: 32 seats
- Each additional HQ floor: +8 seats

The first three expansion steps are intentionally large company milestones. After Corporate HQ, floors can be added indefinitely at escalating cost.

## Save migration

Save schema is v15. Existing SKUs infer their project class from legacy design depth; existing office capacities are preserved and never shrunk.

## Deferred to v1.0.4

The research / capability tree will add explicit technology prerequisites for AA, AAA, categories, buildings and corporate functions. v1.0.3 deliberately establishes the underlying team/time/space mechanics first.
