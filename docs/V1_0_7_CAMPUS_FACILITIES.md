# Business Empire v1.0.7 — Campus & Facilities

This build turns the campus into an active progression system rather than a decorative map.

## Core progression

- Founder Office now progresses through four stages: I (4 positions), II (8), III (16), IV / Corporate HQ (32).
- Founder Office II unlocks the first specialist facilities. Founder III and IV gate more advanced corporate infrastructure.
- Buildings must still touch the connected campus path network.
- Authored isometric building sprites replace the procedural building blocks. While artwork loads, the map shows only a quiet footprint placeholder rather than a procedural building.

## Product development

- Beauty Center: dedicated skincare design center; Level II is required for AAA skincare projects.
- Toy Center: dedicated toy design center; Level II is required for AAA toy projects.
- Research Center: required for company research. Level II is part of the AAA infrastructure gate; upgrades increase research throughput.
- AA projects require Advanced Product Development plus an 8-seat product-capable workspace.
- AAA projects require Flagship Product Development, Research Center II, and the relevant Level II specialist center. The full AAA team must be seated together in that center.

## People

- Training Room can be built early and provides formal employee upskilling slots.
- Level I: 1 simultaneous trainee, 45-day course.
- Level II: 3 simultaneous trainees, 30-day course.
- Training improves role-relevant attributes, skill and morale; employees have a cooldown between formal courses.
- Active courses appear in the People screen, campus building panel and work queue.

## Operations

- Warehouses are repeatable and upgrade Small → Medium → Large.
- Cold Storage supplies climate/refrigerated/frozen infrastructure.
- Logistics Office improves outsourced capacity and production lead times.
- Distribution Hub is late-game high-capacity logistics infrastructure.
- Factory and Sourcing Office remain upgradeable operating facilities.

## Commercial and organization

- Brand Studio improves long-term brand-building efficiency.
- Marketing Office improves campaign execution.
- HR Office speeds recruiting searches.
- Consumer Insights Lab speeds commissioned market studies.
- Executive Wing adds flexible senior seating and a small company-wide leadership bonus.

## Art

New padded transparent sprites are included for:

- Founder Office I / II / III / IV
- Warehouse Small / Medium
- Research Center
- Beauty Center
- Toy Center
- Training Room

The Founder Office I sprite is deliberately rendered smaller on the map so growth into II–IV is visually obvious. Specialist upgrades and Warehouse III use modest scale growth until a later asset pass adds bespoke art for every individual tier.
