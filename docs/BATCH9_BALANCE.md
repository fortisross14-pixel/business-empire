# Batch 9 — Gameplay & Balance Pass

This pass freezes feature expansion and tunes the existing simulation around learnable cause-and-effect.

## Core rule
Cash and marketing buy attempts and awareness; they do not override product-market fit. The market conversion layer combines Product, Price, Channel, Brand and IP fit. A severe mismatch can therefore remain a flop despite heavy spending.

## Difficulty philosophy
Entrepreneur, Standard and Bootstrap share identical customer/competitor logic. They differ in runway and financing pressure, preserving transferable player learning between modes.

## Research loop
The Post-Launch Product Study is intentionally retrospective. A player can launch badly, pay for research, discover why demand failed, and use that information on the next product. Diagnostics are expressed in player-facing 1–5 stars rather than exposing raw engine coefficients.

## Calibration checks used in this pass
A deliberately incoherent skincare case — luxury brand, $200 moisturizer, superhero IP, ValueMart distribution — scores roughly 1/5 Price, 2/5 Channel, 5/5 Brand, 2/5 IP and 4/5 intrinsic Product fit. It is therefore recognized as a potentially reasonable luxury product deployed with the wrong commercial system, not generically labeled a bad product.

Engine strict TypeScript and full-app structural TypeScript checks pass in the build environment. The environment cannot fetch npm packages, so the final Vite bundle cannot be regenerated here without the original dependencies.
