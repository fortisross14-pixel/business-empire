# v2.0 — Three-Industry Expansion

This release makes Packaged Food, Apparel and Consumer Electronics fully playable alongside Skincare and Toys. Each is available as a starting industry and as an organic expansion business.

## Packaged Food

- Products: Breakfast Cereal, Snack Bar, Chips, Cookies, Candy, Ready Meal.
- Consumer priorities: Taste, Health, Convenience, Value, Natural, Licensed / IP.
- Strategy: high repeat purchase and grocery reach can create scale, while expiry, food-safety testing, ingredient volatility and cold storage create operating risk.
- Specialist facility: Food Innovation Kitchen.
- Specialist retailers: FreshBasket and SnackStop.
- Industry event: Ingredient Market Spike.

## Apparel

- Products: T-Shirt, Hoodie, Denim, Activewear, Children's Apparel, Handbag.
- Consumer priorities: Style, Comfort, Durability, Value, Sustainability, Luxury / Prestige, Licensed / IP.
- Strategy: brand and collection direction can support strong pricing power, but trend-driven products and seasonal inventory can age quickly.
- Specialist facility: Fashion Atelier.
- Specialist retailers: StyleHouse and SportCore.
- Industry event: A Trend Breaks Early.

## Consumer Electronics

- Products: Headphones, Wireless Earbuds, Smartwatch, Gaming Controller, Smart Speaker, Home Security.
- Consumer priorities: Performance, Reliability, Ease of Use, Design, Ecosystem, Value, Privacy.
- Strategy: high ticket prices create upside, while complex manufacturing, secure storage, component volatility, safety testing and obsolescence raise the execution bar.
- Specialist facility: Prototype & Reliability Lab.
- Specialist retailers: TechWorld, GameGrid and HomeTech.
- Industry event: Critical Component Shortage.

- 18 new product cards and three authored isometric campus assets.
- Nine compatible manufacturing partners with cost, quality, speed, scale and reliability trade-offs.
- Product-specific materials, manufacturing families, storage profiles, regulation, retail affinity, lifecycle, seasonality, repeat purchase, hit volatility and capabilities.
- Category expansion projects for advanced products and organic entry projects for all five industries.
- Specialist centers accelerate development and gate AAA projects at Level II.
