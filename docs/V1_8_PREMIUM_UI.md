# Business Empire v1.8 — Premium UI & Feedback

v1.8 is a focused presentation release. It makes the existing v1.7 game systems feel
authored, readable and responsive without adding another simulation surface or changing
the save schema.

## Visual language

- The campus rail and overlay navigation use a shared semantic icon family with individual
  gradients, highlight colors and active-state glow.
- Buttons, cards and panels react to hover, press, focus and selection. Epic and legendary
  products receive differentiated portfolio treatments and a light-sweep response.
- Overlays, alerts and achievements use coordinated entrance and exit motion. Players who
  request reduced motion receive immediate, non-animated state changes.

## Product and event moments

Design completion, market launch and breakout alerts use a cinematic mystery-card reveal.
The card spins, reveals the real product visual and then introduces the results and actions.
Players may tap to skip the reveal. Closing a product or competitive moment now animates
before the paused simulation returns to player control.

Decision events visually confirm the selected response before applying it. Unselected
options recede, the selected option resolves, and then the real state-changing choice runs.

## Living campus

The campus map now exposes active work instead of hiding it in menus:

- product design;
- manufacturing;
- research;
- recruiting;
- training; and
- marketing campaigns.

Each live item uses a radial progress indicator, a linear tracker, a percentage and a direct
route to its owning screen. Facility labels also use clearer progress bars and active-state
signals. These indicators derive from the existing work queues and timers.

## Market visualization

The Market screen now opens with a compact command center:

- live daily, monthly and yearly share;
- a competitive-share donut and ranked company list;
- a quarterly player-versus-rivals share race; and
- trailing revenue and profit lines with event markers.

The charts use the same competitive reviews, share histories, financial history and events
already stored by the simulation. They are visual explanations of authoritative state, not
decorative placeholder data.

## Compatibility

v1.8 keeps save schema 20 and remains compatible with v1.7 companies. The release changes
presentation components and derives visual progress from existing state; it introduces no
new required save fields.
