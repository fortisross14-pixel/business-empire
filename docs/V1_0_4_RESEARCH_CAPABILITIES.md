# Business Empire v1.0.4 — Research & Capability Tree

## Purpose

The player should feel that the company has to learn how to become larger. Money buys the project, people accelerate it, and research unlocks the organizational capability itself.

## Capability branches

### Product
- Advanced Product Development → AA projects
- Flagship Product Development → AAA projects

### Organization
- People & HR Foundations → online recruiting
- Executive Search → deep recruiting
- Organizational Scaling → 16-seat Large Office
- Corporate Headquarters → 32-seat Massive Corporate HQ
- Vertical Expansion → repeatable +8-seat HQ floors

### Operations
- Supplier Management → dedicated Sourcing Office
- Owned Manufacturing → Factory / owned production

### Market
- Market Intelligence → enables the intelligence function; staffed Strategy/Intelligence seats are still required to operate it.

## Research economy

Only one corporate capability project runs at once. Every project has a cash commitment and a required development-point total. The Founder supplies a baseline 1 point/day; Product, Strategy and Operations teams accelerate the rate according to their effectiveness. This means a tiny startup can progress eventually, while a mature organization researches faster because it has accumulated real organizational depth.

## Category development

Existing category-entry projects remain data-driven by each product archetype, but the player-facing action is centralized under Company → Research. Brands display what they can use; Research is where the company develops new category capability.

## Save migration

Schema v16 adds ResearchState. Existing saves are not reset: capabilities are grandfathered from evidence already present in the company (AA/AAA products, office scale, factories, sourcing offices, recruiting state and intelligence departments).
