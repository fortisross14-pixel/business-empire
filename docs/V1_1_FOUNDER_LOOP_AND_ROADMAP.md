# Business Empire v1.1 — Founder Loop & Company Roadmap

## Intent

v1.1 makes the first commercial loop easier to understand and makes company
growth legible without flattening the simulation into an automatic tutorial.
It adds no save-breaking state and does not simplify the underlying product,
capacity, research or personnel systems.

## Founder Loop

- Founder Goals now lead with a focused **First Revenue Plan**: design → first
  batch → commercial plan → release → first customers.
- The Product Sell stage has a **Launch Readiness** panel. It scores inventory,
  selected channels, a seated Marketing owner and category-appropriate price,
  then explains the next missing commercial decision.
- A company’s first launch now gets a small **founder-led discovery push**. It
  is weighted toward product-fit customer cells and does not override price,
  channel, quality or product-market fit. This removes the zero-awareness dead
  zone that could make a fully prepared first launch appear to do nothing.

## Company Roadmap

Company → Roadmap is the new canonical map for larger ambitions. It turns each
strategic goal into concrete prerequisites and links every incomplete step to
the screen where it can be acted on.

1. First revenue engine
2. Company development base (space, Research Center, CIO, first capability)
3. AA product program
4. Talent engine
5. Owned supply chain
6. AAA flagship program

The roadmap is guidance, not an enforced linear campaign. A player may still
choose a different investment order; each card simply makes the real gates
visible before they spend money or wait on a project.

## Compatibility

- Save schema unchanged.
- Existing companies see the roadmap derived from their current state.
- No product, facility, personnel or research record is migrated or modified.
