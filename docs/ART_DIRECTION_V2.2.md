# Business Empire v2.2 art direction

## North star: Executive Playset

The interface combines a deep-navy management-game shell with warm white working
surfaces, saturated blue/violet/green actions, readable rounded typography and
toy-like 3D business objects. Screens should feel optimistic, tactile and immediately
playable—not like an enterprise dashboard.

## Generated asset prompt set

All images were created with OpenAI's built-in image generator. The supplied concept
screens were used only as broad style references; the generated scenes contain no
third-party logos, text or copied interface elements.

1. **Campus main menu** — a polished stylized 3D business-tycoon campus diorama,
   optimistic afternoon light, colorful headquarters, factory, research studio,
   warehouse, roads, landscaping and delivery vehicles; strong depth and clean toy-like
   materials; active visual interest on the right with calmer negative space on the left;
   no words, UI, logos or watermarks.
2. **Product studio** — a bright premium product-development studio containing generic
   consumer-product prototypes, packaging mockups, material boards and a small assembly
   area; polished 3D game art, blue/violet accents, cinematic depth, open center for UI;
   no words, logos or watermarks.
3. **Market command** — a vibrant management-game market command room overlooking a
   shopping district, with abstract physical chart forms, customer clusters and retail
   activity; deep blue and cyan palette with warm accents, readable negative space;
   no words, logos or watermarks.
4. **Produce action** — an isolated toy-like 3D miniature factory with conveyor and
   shipping crates, saturated blue/cyan, transparent background, no text or logos.
5. **Campaign action** — an isolated toy-like 3D megaphone with colorful customer
   silhouettes and sparkle accents, blue/magenta palette, transparent background, no text.
6. **Market-study action** — an isolated toy-like 3D magnifying glass over a pie-chart
   token and customer markers, cyan/orange/violet, transparent background, no text.
7. **New-version action** — an isolated toy-like 3D product prototype, design board,
   pencil and upward iteration arrow, blue/violet palette, transparent background, no text.

## Asset locations

- `public/assets/ui/backgrounds/`
- `public/assets/ui/actions/`
- `public/assets/ui/manifest.json`

Generated raster art should support the interface hierarchy; live simulation data,
charts, product labels and controls remain native React/CSS so they stay legible and
responsive.
