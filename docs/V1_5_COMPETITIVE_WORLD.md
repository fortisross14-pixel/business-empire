# v1.5 — Competitive World

Competition is now a visible, persistent commercial story.

## Competitive command center

The Market navigation contains a dedicated Competition screen with three jobs:

- **Segment battles** explain who leads each saved audience, the player's position and share,
  the products committed to the fight, the strategic situation and a concrete response.
- **Rival dossiers** combine identity, posture, product portfolio, pricing, quality, market
  position, momentum and durable strategic actions.
- **Market news** collects rival actions, quarterly reviews, product events and shocks into a
  readable commercial timeline.

## Rival memory

Competitors preserve their last 32 meaningful actions. Launches into a player stronghold,
defensive marketing and retreats are recorded independently from the rolling notification
queue. Quarterly share history is retained for 24 quarters.

## Executive moments

A rival launch into a player stronghold pauses play with a Rival Move briefing. Every quarter
also ends with a paused Market Review showing position, share, movement and the leader. Both
cards link directly into Competition and leave the game paused until the player chooses to
resume.

## Save compatibility

Save schema v19 adds competitive reviews and initializes durable rival histories on existing
companies. Existing AI behavior remains intact and is now surfaced and explained rather than
replaced. No stock market, acquisitions or fundraising systems are introduced in this batch.
