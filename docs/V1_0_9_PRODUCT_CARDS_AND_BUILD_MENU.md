# v1.0.9 – Product Card Readability + Campus Build Menu Clarity

## What changed

### Product portfolio cards
- Enlarged the product hero visual so the product is recognizable at a glance.
- Reduced wasted white space inside cards.
- Increased the size and legibility of the brand bubble.
- Kept the stage pill visible while making the card more product-first.
- Scaled the portfolio grid up from small 260px cards to roomier 330px cards.

### Product art framing
- Updated `ProductVisualCard` and `ProductPackIcon` to use a larger central illustration area.
- Improved the top badges and lower metadata row for faster scanning.

### Campus build menu
- Reorganized the build picker into clearer category panels.
- Added short blurbs for each category.
- Sorted buildable facilities to the top of each section.
- Added READY / LOCKED status labels.
- Surfaced the missing requirement directly in locked items.

## Intent
This pass focuses on usability and visual communication rather than adding a large new asset batch. The goal is to make it much easier to understand what can be built next and to recognize products instantly from the portfolio view.
