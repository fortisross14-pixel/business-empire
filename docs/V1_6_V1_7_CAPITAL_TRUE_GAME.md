# Business Empire v1.6–v1.7

## v1.6: Lightweight Capital

The Capital Desk intentionally keeps financing legible. The player can use a revolving
credit line, request a larger growth loan, connect with one of three investor archetypes,
or raise equity. The screen shows company value, runway, interest and founder ownership so
the consequence is visible before the button is pressed.

Equity valuation responds to company value, investor relationship and confidence. Debt is
constrained by value and confidence. The system does not attempt to simulate cap tables,
covenant documents, stock markets or acquisition finance.

## v1.7: Scenarios, Decisions and Legacy

New companies choose one of five scenarios:

- Bootstrap Brand
- Premium Challenger
- Turnaround
- Retailer-Dependent Growth
- IP Breakout

Each has three goals surfaced in Founder Goals and the Achievements & Legacy screen.

The decision framework supports state-aware triggers, two or three explicit choices,
immediate effects, delayed consequences and permanent history. The first event set covers
supplier shocks, retailer pressure, viral demand, quality incidents, employee retention,
competitor attacks, trend shifts and funding pressure. Events pause the game only when a
choice is required.

Fifteen achievements cover the path from first facility and first sale to market leadership,
original IP, a second business and a three-year company. Six run outcomes recognize
profitable scale, category leadership, a multi-business group, iconic IP, an exit-ready
company and restructuring failure. Outcomes never end the save; sandbox play continues.

## Compatibility

Save schema 20 migrates existing companies into the new systems without changing their
cash or debt. Existing games receive their first decision after a grace period. Capital,
events, delayed effects, achievements and outcomes are all serialized.

## Next release

v1.8 should be a focused GUI and asset-polish release: stronger authored art, richer motion,
improved transitions, higher-impact moment presentation, better iconography, refined map
materials and a consistent audio/feedback layer. It should polish the systems now present
rather than add another large simulation surface.
